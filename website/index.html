About Bristol Community Events

Founded in 2023, Bristol Community Events (BCE) is the city's official platform for discovering and booking local events — from live music and theatre to workshops, sports and community gatherings.

As an official partner of UWE Bristol, we are committed to making Bristol's cultural scene accessible to everyone. All students receive a 10% discount across every ticketed event on our platform.
