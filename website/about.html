About Bristol Community Events

Promoting tourism and bringing Bristol's communities together through events.

Our Mission

Founded in 2023, Bristol Community Events (BCE) is a platform dedicated to promoting tourism and community engagement across Bristol. From exhibitions and workshops to music, sports, theatre and religious events — BCE brings everything happening in the city into one place.

We work with venues, organisers and communities across Bristol to provide seamless event discovery and ticket booking for residents, students and visitors alike.

As an official partner of UWE Bristol, we are committed to making Bristol's cultural scene accessible to everyone — including a 10% student discount across all ticketed events.
